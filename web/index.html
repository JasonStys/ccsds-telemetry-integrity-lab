USER GUIDE / TRUST BOUNDARIES

What the evidence can—and cannot—tell you.

Inspect a capture

Choose a synthetic scenario and run inspection. Click a record to read the six-byte primary header and opaque payload. Filters affect the ledger only; totals and exports retain every record.

Review continuity

Counts wrap modulo 16384 and are tracked per APID in one capture/session. Gaps, late/reset ambiguity, exact baseline repeats and counter reuse remain distinct. Review flags are not automatic operational decisions.

Understand recovery

Each newline in the lab hex capture supplies a trusted record boundary. A malformed record cannot swallow the next one. Raw CCSDS byte-stream resynchronization, secondary headers, segmentation and transfer frames are not implemented.

Protect your data

No accounts, analytics, ads, uploads, runtime APIs or live systems. CLI-only local imports and optional SQL history stay on your device. Preferences save only controls when you opt in. Use synthetic, non-sensitive captures.
